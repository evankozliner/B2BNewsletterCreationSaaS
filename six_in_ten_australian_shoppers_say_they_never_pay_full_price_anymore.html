Six in ten Australian consumers say they never buy at full price anymore

Australia Post's latest quarterly ecommerce report found Australians spent $21.9 billion online last quarter, up 14% year on year. But average basket sizes dropped to a record low of $90 as shoppers hold out for sales events and trade down to smaller purchases.

SEO Strategy

Structured data and semantic HTML do different jobs, and your site probably neglects one of them

Teams often treat structured data and semantic HTML as interchangeable. Semantic HTML tells crawlers how a page is organised. Structured data tells them who published it, what it costs and whether it is in stock. A page needs both, implemented in the right order.

Why It Matters: If your site uses a page builder, the HTML may look right but lack real structure underneath. View-source one product or service page this week and check whether headings use real heading tags. Fixing this once removes a structural drag that quietly limits rankings regardless of how much content you publish or how many links you build.

Platform Update

WPForms Lite’s setup wizard redirects 5 million WordPress sites to an external server during installation

A security researcher found that WPForms Lite’s onboarding wizard silently sends new users to an external site and installs extra plugins without clear consent. The plugin issues a one-hour admin access token during setup. It runs on over 5 million WordPress sites.

Why It Matters: If your WordPress site uses WPForms Lite, check what was installed alongside it. Deactivate anything you did not choose. Ten minutes protects your admin access.

SE Ranking analysed 50,000 prompts and found ChatGPT shows ads on 26% of commercial queries

ChatGPT serves a single sponsored placement below its generated answer on roughly one in four commercial prompts. But 14% of those ads appear alongside unrelated topics, and buying an ad does not increase a brand’s chance of being cited in the AI answer above it.

Why It Matters: ChatGPT Ads is a new paid channel approaching Google AI Mode’s ad frequency. If you run Google Ads, add ChatGPT Ads to your watchlist this quarter and test a small budget. Early movers on a new channel pay less before competition drives costs up.

The One Thing

Open your WordPress plugin list and count the ones you do not recognise.

Deactivate and delete any plugin you are not actively using. Each unused plugin is an unmonitored door into your site. The WPForms story this week is a reminder: even popular plugins can behave in ways you did not agree to. Ten minutes, one less attack surface.
